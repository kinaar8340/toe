# Analog suspicion vs physical suspicion

```
MODEL, not theorem.
Geometry and arithmetic stay on QGA / flux_hopf_lib.
The alkane / CH2 / carbene language is an analogy for a discrete insertion step.
n=1 is one flywheel at quaternion identity (methane slot).
n=2 is one extra published step: one extra flywheel XOR one extra rotor insertion.
Do not emit “proves”, “element”, “periodic table identity”, or “carbene is a flywheel”.
```

The analog suspicion was correct. The physical one was not tested.

## What the runs confirmed

The homolog experiment splits by **insertion-axis rule**, not by alkane labels. \(n\) is a step count with \(Z\) frozen. That is exactly what happened.

- Rotor is a fixed-axis golden-angle walk on \(S^3\). Identity overlap is \(\lvert\cos((n-1)\theta/2)\rvert\).
- Flywheel records bake-\(x\) and does not rotate the slot quaternion, so overlap stays \(1\).
- Published shares rotor at \(n=2\) (one rotor, \(\lvert\mathrm{Re}\,q\rvert\) depends only on \(\theta\)), then diverges because the axes do not commute.
- Off-\(yz\), \(S^2\) drift is the chord \(\arccos(\cos^2\varphi+\sin^2\varphi\cos\theta)\), not \(\theta\). `walk_phase` stays \(\theta\). Overlap at \(n=4\) moves because the plane sequence changed.

Those numbers match an independent composition of unit quaternions. The analog is doing the geometry it claimed to do.

## What that does not confirm

Correct analog results mean the discrete rule is consistent on \(S^3\). They do not mean:

- a homolog series is a flywheel stack
- CH₂ or carbene is an insertion rotor
- \(n\) is an element or \(Z\)
- the periodic table is this walk
- the magnetic-orbital maps validate the TOE

The aliases were the same function of \(n\) in every mode. That was a bookkeeping check, not evidence that the names are the objects.

## How to hold the two suspicions apart

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Three axis rules produce three different walks | Confirmed by the analog |
| Published is a walking-plane product, rotor is a one-parameter subgroup | Confirmed |
| \(S^2\) chord \(\neq\) generator angle unless \(v\perp\) bake-\(x\) | Confirmed |
| This identifies chemistry or the vacuum lattice | Not tested, and the banner forbids saying it |

So: suspicion about the **model analog** — yes. Suspicion that the analog is the thing it is named after — still only a naming choice. Keep geometry on QGA / `flux_hopf_lib`, and treat these CSVs as maps of a quaternion insertion rule.
